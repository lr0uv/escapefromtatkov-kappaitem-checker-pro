    How to use
    1. Tap an item once you have handed it in to check it off.
    2. Progress is saved in this browser automatically. No sign-up needed.
    3. To use the same progress on PC and phone, create a secret key at the bottom of the page.
    Your progress is only saved in this browser

    If the browser data is cleared, your progress goes with it. Create a secret key to restore it on any device or browser.

    Safari on iPhone/iPad may delete site data after 7 days without a visit. Use Share > Add to Home Screen to keep it safer.

      About the Kappa container

      The Secure container Kappa is the reward for Fence's "Collector" task. You must hand over every listed item found in raid (FIR).

      The list is pulled automatically from tarkov.dev, so new items added by patches show up on their own.
